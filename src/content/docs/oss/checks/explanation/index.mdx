Understanding-oriented articles that explain the _why_ behind Giskard Checks design decisions.

These pages are background reading rather than instructions: nothing here is a
step you follow, and no page assumes you are in the middle of a task. Start with
[Core Concepts](/oss/checks/explanation/core-concepts), which introduces the
primitives — Interaction, Trace, Check, Scenario — that the rest of the
documentation takes for granted. From there, [When to Use Which
Check](/oss/checks/explanation/when-to-use-which-check) covers how to choose
between rule-based, semantic, and LLM-as-judge validation, while the remaining
two pages explain the async execution model and the JSONPath syntax used to pull
values out of a trace.
