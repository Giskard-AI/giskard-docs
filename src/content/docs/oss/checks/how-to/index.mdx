Task-oriented guides for writing tests with Giskard Checks. These guides assume you're familiar with scenarios and checks; the terms used here are introduced in [Core Concepts](/oss/checks/explanation/core-concepts). Each guide focuses on getting a specific job done.

The guides are ordered roughly by how soon you are likely to need them rather
than by difficulty, and each one stands alone: read the single page that matches
your problem instead of working through the section.
[Run Tests with pytest](/oss/checks/how-to/run-in-pytest) is the usual starting
point, since most of the other guides assume your checks already run under
pytest. From there, pick what you need — simulating users, spying on internal
calls, validating structured output, evaluating in batches, or writing checks of
your own — and finish with
[CI/CD Integration](/oss/checks/how-to/ci-cd) once the tests are worth running
on every commit.
