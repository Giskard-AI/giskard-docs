These are worked examples — not tutorials or how-to guides. They show a complete system being built and tested end-to-end, applying Giskard Checks to real AI application domains. Best read after [Tutorials](/oss/checks/tutorials) or [How-to Guides](/oss/checks/how-to).

Each page below builds a small but complete system and then tests it, so you can
see how scenarios, checks, and traces come together on a realistic application
rather than a toy example. They are independent — read the one closest to what
you are building. [RAG Evaluation](/oss/checks/use-cases/rag-evaluation) is the
most thorough if you want a sense of what a full suite looks like, and
[Content Moderation](/oss/checks/use-cases/content-moderation) is the shortest
if you mainly care about safety checks.
