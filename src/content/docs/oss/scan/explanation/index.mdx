Background reading for when the report says something surprising and you want to know why.

A scan that finds nothing does not mean your agent is safe. It means these generated scenarios did not break it. These pages explain what the scan does and does not cover. For definitions of the vulnerability names in the report, see the [glossary](/start/glossary).

Read **How the Scan Works** first. It walks through the two phases of a scan, generating the scenarios and then running them, and explains the pieces involved: the description you write, the generators that turn it into test cases, the scenarios themselves, and the LLM judge that decides pass or fail. **What the Scan Looks For** is the companion reference for reading a report: it lists the tags the scan attaches to each finding, so you can tell a threat type from a component and see which generator produced what.
